# Rvised - Your YouTube Learning Companion

## 🎯 Product Overview

**Rvised** is a powerful Chrome extension and web application that transforms YouTube into your personal learning platform. Using advanced AI technology, Rvised instantly generates comprehensive summaries, extracts key insights, and helps you organize your learning journey - all while saving you hours of time.

## ✨ Core Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Summaries
- **Instant Generation**: Get comprehensive summaries of any YouTube video in seconds
- **Smart Extraction**: Automatically identifies main takeaways, key insights, and action items
- **Multiple Formats**: Choose between bullet points, detailed analysis, or quick overviews
- **Accuracy**: Powered by OpenAI's latest models for high-quality, accurate summaries

### 📚 Smart Organization System
- **Learning Projects**: Create themed collections to organize related videos (max 30 videos per project)
- **Project Management**: Easy creation, editing, and deletion of projects
- **Visual Organization**: Beautiful project cards with emojis and color coding
- **Progress Tracking**: Monitor your learning progress within each project



### 📊 Professional Dashboard
- **Learning Analytics**: Track your streak, total videos summarized, and time saved
- **Recent Activity**: Quick access to your latest summaries
- **Project Overview**: See all your active projects at a glance
- **Motivational Insights**: Personalized messages based on your learning patterns
- **Beautiful UI**: Clean, modern interface inspired by leading productivity apps

### 📖 Library Management
- **Grid & List Views**: Switch between visual layouts based on preference
- **Advanced Search**: Find summaries by title, content, or keywords
- **Smart Filtering**: Filter by date, project, or custom criteria
- **Sort Options**: Organize by recent, alphabetical, or most viewed
- **Quick Stats**: See total summaries, hours saved, and storage metrics

### 📥 Export & Sharing
- **Download as Text**: Export summaries as formatted text files for offline access
- **Copy to Clipboard**: One-click copying of summaries
- **Structured Format**: Professional formatting with sections for insights, takeaways, and action items
- **Batch Operations**: Download multiple summaries at once

### 🎨 User Experience
- **Seamless Integration**: Non-intrusive overlay that appears on YouTube videos
- **One-Click Access**: Simple activation with the Rvised button
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all screen sizes
- **Dark Mode Ready**: Comfortable viewing in any lighting condition
- **Smooth Animations**: Polished transitions and interactions
